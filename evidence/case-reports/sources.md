- Changed on rereading (2026-11-03), each where the second rater differed by more than 3 points. The reason is the page text, not the rater's score:
  - ICSE 2003, 12.5 to 11: objectivity 3.5 to 2. A call reports no data, so conclusions cannot follow it (0), and its organisers have an interest in submissions (half).
  - USENIX 2010, 11 to 10: objectivity 3 to 2, for the same reasons. It stays at the threshold.
  - Hashimoto 2026, 10.5 to 8.5: authority 3 to 1. The page names no organisation. It names Ghostty, the author's own product, as other work (half), and states experience (half). The earlier 3 used knowledge of the author from outside the page.
  - Git 2026, 12 to 10.5: authority 4 to 3, objectivity 3.5 to 2.5, date 0.5 to 1. The page has no named author (other work 0). A reference page has no conclusions (0), and the project documents its own tool (half). The release history gives dates for the version shown (1).
- A company post about its own work is its owner's claim about itself: Carlini 2026 and Lin 2026 carry a vested interest, scored as such.
- Hashimoto 2026 reports no measurement; its numbers are self-reported.
